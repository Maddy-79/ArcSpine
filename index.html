Settings

Personalize feedback and local data.

Posture sensitivity

Posture status flips the instant you cross the warning angle — safe zone just keeps small natural sway from triggering an alert, with no extra waiting period. Buzz duration is how long the vibration motor pulses, once, when a strain is detected — it stops on its own and won't buzz again until you're back to OK and slouch again. Both settings are sent to the ESP32 so the vibration motor and the app agree.

Notifications & buzzer

Turn the buzzer off if the vibration gets annoying — the browser notification alone will still remind you.

Local data
